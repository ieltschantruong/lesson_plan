Listening Section

Section 1 & 2 Strategy Guide

🎯 Section 1 Reminder

  • 1. Format: A conversation between two speakers in an everyday social context (e.g., booking a room).
  • 2. Question Type: Usually form completion, note completion, or table completion.
  • 3. Focus: Listen for specific factual details like names, numbers, dates, times, and places. Expect names to be spelled out.
  • 4. Action: Always check the word limit instruction (e.g., "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS").

💡 Section 2 Instructions

  • 1. Format: A monologue (one person speaking) set in an everyday social context (e.g., a speech about local facilities).
  • 2. Question Types: Often includes multiple-choice (single or multiple answers), map/plan labeling.
  • 3. Multiple Choice Strategy: Use preparation time to highlight keywords in the question stem and core differences between options. Expect to hear distractors.
  • 4. Map Labeling Strategy: Locate the starting point. Pay close attention to directional language (next to, opposite, north).

Speaking Part 1

Study & Work

Exercise 1: Vocabulary in Context

"Currently I'm a professional chef, working in a 5 Michelin Star restaurant. When I was a little boy, I aspired to be a qualified doctor to treat patients. However, after three times failing the university entrance exam to a medical college, I got discouraged, so I made up my mind to pursue the same job as my parents, and to my surprise, I realized I had a knack for cooking. Although being a chef can be at times stressful and demanding because it requires a high level of meticulousness, I still have a burning passion for it."
1 currently (adv) at the present time
2 professional (adj) engaged in a specified activity as a main paid occupation
3 aspire to do something (v) have a strong desire to achieve something
4 qualified (adj) officially recognized as being trained to perform a particular job
5 make up one's mind to do sth (v) make a decision
6 pursue (v) follow or try to achieve something
7 have a knack for doing sth (v) have a natural skill or ability
8 stressful (adj) causing mental or emotional stress
9 demanding (adj) requiring much skill or effort
10 meticulousness (n) great attention to detail
11 have a burning passion for sth (v) have a very strong interest or love for something

Speaking Questions

STUDY

  1. What is your major? / Why did you choose to study that major?
  2. Do you like your major? / What do you like about your school?
  3. What was your favorite subject as a child? / What is your favorite subject now?
  4. Is there anything you don't like about your school?

WORK

  1. What do you do? (What is your favorite job?)
  2. Why did you choose this job?
  3. What are some advantages and disadvantages of doing this job?
  4. Have you ever wanted to change your job?
💡 View Sample Answers ▼

STUDY: Do you like your major?

Yes, I have a burning passion for it. Even though it is quite demanding, I truly believe I have a knack for dealing with complex problems.

WORK: What do you do?

Currently, I am a professional graphic designer. I aspired to work in this field since I was young, so I made up my mind to pursue it.

WORK: Advantages and disadvantages?

The main advantage is doing what I love. However, the deadlines can be incredibly stressful, and the clients expect a high degree of meticulousness from a qualified designer.

Speaking Questions

TRAVEL

  1. Do you like to travel?
  2. Do you often travel by plane?
  3. What is the best place that you have been to?
  4. Which country would you like to travel to in the future?
  5. Would you like to travel to space?
💡 View Sample Answers ▼

1. Do you like to travel?

Yes, absolutely. Whenever I feel stressed, I yearn to escape the city and travel somewhere to unwind and recharge my batteries.

3. What is the best place that you have been to?

I loved visiting a remote mountain village. It was completely off the beaten track, which gave me the perfect opportunity to savor local delicacies without the crowds.

4. Which country would you like to travel to in the future?

I would love to visit Japan. Though I am a student on a limited budget, I think a discerning traveler can still find affordable recreational pastimes there.

Speaking Questions

TRANSPORT

  1. What's the most popular means of transportation in your hometown?
  2. How often do you take buses?
  3. Can you compare the advantages of planes and trains?
  4. Is driving to work popular in your country?
  5. Do you think people will drive more in the future?
  6. Would you ride bikes to work in the future?
  7. What will become the most popular means of transportation in Vietnam?
💡 View Sample Answers ▼

1. What's the most popular means of transportation in your hometown?

Motorbikes are definitely the most popular. They are a quick way to get to a destination through heavy traffic, although you aren't protected from the weather.

3. Can you compare the advantages of planes and trains?

Planes are much faster for long distances, but they are often more expensive. Trains are more scenic and comfortable, but you are heavily restricted by timetables.

6. Would you ride bikes to work in the future?

I would consider it if the city builds safer bike lanes. Cycling is highly environmentally friendly and very healthy, but safety is a concern currently.
