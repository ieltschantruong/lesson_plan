Listening Section

Section 1 Strategy Guide

🎯 Understanding Section 1

  • 1.
    Format: A conversation between two speakers in an everyday social context (e.g., booking a room, renting a car, making an inquiry).
  • 2.
    Question Type: Almost exclusively form completion, note completion, or table completion.
  • 3.
    Focus: Listen for specific factual details like names, numbers, dates, times, prices, and places. Expect names of people and streets to be spelled out letter-by-letter.
  • 4.
    Action: Always check the word limit instruction carefully (e.g., "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER"). Hyphenated words count as one word.
  • 5.
    Distractors: Be careful of "self-correction". A speaker might give a number, but the other speaker corrects them, or they change their mind immediately after.

Speaking Questions

STUDY

  1. What is your major? / Why did you choose to study that major?
  2. Do you like your major? / What do you like about your school?
  3. What was your favorite subject as a child? / What is your favorite subject now?
  4. Is there anything you don't like about your school?

WORK

  1. What do you do? (What is your favorite job?)
  2. Why did you choose this job?
  3. What are some advantages and disadvantages of doing this job?
  4. Have you ever wanted to change your job?
💡 View Sample Answers ▼

STUDY: What is your major? / Why did you choose to study that major?

Currently, I am a senior majoring in Marketing. I made up my mind to pursue this field because I realized I have a knack for creative thinking and understanding consumer behavior.

STUDY: Do you like your major?

Yes, I have a burning passion for it. Even though it is quite demanding, I truly believe I am well-suited for dealing with complex marketing problems.

STUDY: What was your favorite subject as a child? / What is your favorite subject now?

As a child, I always aspired to be good at math, but my favorite was actually literature. Now, my favorite is digital marketing because it requires a high level of meticulousness and practical application.

STUDY: Is there anything you don't like about your school?

The only thing I dislike is the exam period. The schedule can be incredibly stressful and demanding, leaving me exhausted sometimes.

WORK: What do you do?

Currently, I am a professional graphic designer. I aspired to work in this field since I was young, so I made up my mind to pursue it.

WORK: Why did you choose this job?

I chose it because I have a burning passion for visual arts. I also discovered early on that I have a knack for combining colors and typography, so it felt like the natural path to become a qualified designer.

WORK: Advantages and disadvantages?

The main advantage is doing what I love. However, the deadlines can be incredibly stressful, and the clients expect a high degree of meticulousness and demanding revisions.

WORK: Have you ever wanted to change your job?

Not currently. I am quite satisfied where I am. However, if the environment becomes too stressful without proper compensation, I might make up my mind to seek a new path.

Speaking Questions

TRAVEL

  1. Do you like to travel?
  2. Do you often travel by plane?
  3. What is the best place that you have been to?
  4. Which country would you like to travel to in the future?
  5. Would you like to travel to space?
💡 View Sample Answers ▼

1. Do you like to travel?

Yes, absolutely. Whenever I feel stressed, I yearn to escape the city and travel somewhere to unwind and recharge my batteries.

2. Do you often travel by plane?

Not very often, to be honest. Since I am usually traveling on a limited budget, I tend to use trains or buses. I only take a plane when I travel internationally as a discerning traveler seeking new experiences.

3. What is the best place that you have been to?

I loved visiting a remote mountain village. It was completely off the beaten track, which gave me the perfect opportunity to savor local delicacies without the crowds.

4. Which country would you like to travel to in the future?

I would love to visit Japan. Though I am a student on a limited budget, I think there are still plenty of affordable recreational pastimes to enjoy there.

5. Would you like to travel to space?

It sounds like a fascinating experience, but personally, no. I would rather stay on Earth to unwind and savor the natural beauty here rather than take on such an extreme and risky journey.

Speaking Questions

TRANSPORT

  1. What's the most popular means of transportation in your hometown?
  2. How often do you take buses?
  3. Can you compare the advantages of planes and trains?
  4. Is driving to work popular in your country?
  5. Do you think people will drive more in the future?
  6. Would you ride bikes to work in the future?
  7. What will become the most popular means of transportation in Vietnam?
💡 View Sample Answers ▼

1. What's the most popular means of transportation in your hometown?

Motorbikes are definitely the most popular. They are a quick way to get to a destination through heavy traffic, although you aren't protected from the weather.

2. How often do you take buses?

I rarely take them, mainly because they are heavily restricted by timetables and sometimes you wait a long time. I prefer the flexibility of riding my own motorbike.

3. Can you compare the advantages of planes and trains?

Planes are a quick way to get to a destination for long distances, but they are often more expensive. Trains are more scenic and comfortable, but you are restricted by timetables.

4. Is driving to work popular in your country?

Yes, it's increasingly popular for those who can afford it because you are protected from the weather. However, since cars are expensive to buy, fuel, and insure, and parking can be a problem, motorbikes still dominate.

5. Do you think people will drive more in the future?

I suspect so. As the economy grows, more people will want the comfort of a car. However, it's not very environmentally friendly and might make traffic worse if infrastructure doesn't improve.

6. Would you ride bikes to work in the future?

I would consider it if the city builds safer bike lanes. Cycling is highly environmentally friendly and very healthy, but safety is a concern currently.

7. What will become the most popular means of transportation in Vietnam?

I believe public transport like metro systems will eventually take over. They are environmentally friendly and a reliable way to travel without worrying about traffic, even though you might be restricted by timetables initially.
