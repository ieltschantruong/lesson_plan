Reading Section

Test Overview & Vocabulary

🔎 IELTS Reading Format (Computer-Based)

Timing & Structure

  • • 60 Minutes total. (No transfer time!)
  • • 3 Sections (Passages).
  • • 40 Questions in total.

Computer Features

  • • Split Screen: Text on the left, questions on the right. They scroll independently.
  • • Tools: Right-click to Highlight text or Make Notes.
  • • Shortcuts: Use Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V to avoid spelling errors in gap-fills.

Question Interactions

  • • Multiple Choice: Click the radio buttons.
  • • Matching: Drag and drop the options into the gaps.
  • • Gap Fill: Type directly into the blanks.

🎯 Strategy: True / False / Not Given

  • 1. TRUE: The statement completely agrees with the information in the passage (look for synonyms, not exact matches).
  • 2. FALSE: The statement directly contradicts the facts in the passage.
  • 3. NOT GIVEN: There is no information on this, or it's impossible to confirm based only on the text. Never use outside knowledge!
  • 4. Keywords: Pay close attention to qualifying words (e.g., some, all, mainly, often, always), as they can change the meaning entirely.

✍️ Strategy: Fill-in-the-Blanks

  • 1. Word Limit: Strictly follow the instructions (e.g., "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS"). Hyphenated words count as one.
  • 2. Grammar Predict: Guess the required part of speech (noun, verb, adjective) before scanning. The completed sentence must be grammatically correct.
  • 3. Locate Keywords: Scan the passage using unique names, numbers, or unparaphrasable terms from the question.
  • 4. Computer Trick: Double-click the target word in the text and use Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V to drop it in the blank to avoid spelling errors.

Listening Section

Overview & Complete IELTS SB | Unit 1

🔎 IELTS Listening Format (Computer-Based) & Section 1

General Format

  • • Timing: ~30 mins (+ 2 mins checking time). No 10-min transfer!
  • • Structure: 4 Parts, 40 questions total.
  • • Audio: Played ONLY ONCE.

Computer Features

  • • Volume: Adjust it yourself using the slider at the top right.
  • • Navigation: Use the Tab key to quickly jump to the next gap.
  • • Review: Time to review answers is given between sections.

Section 1 Focus

  • • Context: Everyday social situation (e.g., booking a hotel).
  • • Speakers: A conversation between 2 people.
  • • Objective: Listen for specific factual info (names, numbers, dates).

🎯 Strategy: Form/Note Completion

  • 1. Word Limit: Always read the limit strictly (e.g., "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER").
  • 2. Predict: Guess the missing information type (noun, verb, number, name, price) before listening.
  • 3. Spelling: Names of people or places are often spelled out. Pay attention to double letters.

✅ Strategy: Multiple Choice

  • 1. Pre-read: Skim questions and options quickly during the preparation time.
  • 2. Keywords: Highlight/underline the main differences between the options (A, B, C).
  • 3. Distractors: Beware! Speakers often mention ALL options, but only one is the correct answer to the specific question.
