Reading Section

Question Type Strategies

🎯 Strategy: True / False / Not Given

  • 1. TRUE: The statement completely agrees with the information in the passage. Look for synonyms and matching meanings, not exact words.
  • 2. FALSE: The statement directly contradicts or is the opposite of the facts in the passage.
  • 3. NOT GIVEN: There is no information on this, or it's impossible to confirm based only on the text. Don't assume or use outside knowledge.
  • 4. Keywords Alert: Pay strict attention to qualifying words (e.g., some, all, mainly, often, never, always), as they completely change the meaning.

✍️ Strategy: Fill-in-the-Blanks

  • 1. Check Instructions: Strictly follow the word limit (e.g., "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS"). Hyphenated words count as one.
  • 2. Predict Grammar: Guess the required part of speech (noun, verb, adjective) before scanning. The completed sentence must be grammatically correct.
  • 3. Scan for Keywords: Locate the section using unique names, numbers, or terms from the question that cannot be paraphrased.
  • 4. Computer-Based Tip: Double-click the target word in the passage and use Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V to copy-paste it into the blank. This prevents silly spelling errors.

Practice: Accommodation

💬 Part 1

  1. What kind of housing/accommodation do you live in?
  2. Who do you live with?
  3. Do you plan to live there for a long time?
  4. Do you prefer living in a house or a flat?
  5. What part of your home do you like the most?
💡 View P1 Sample Answers ▼

1. What kind of housing/accommodation do you live in?

Currently, I live in a spacious, single-storey house located in a quiet residential area.

2. Who do you live with?

I live with my nuclear family, including my parents and my younger brother. We actually just threw a house-warming party last month!

3. Do you plan to live there for a long time?

Yes, I do. The decor perfectly suits my taste for minimalism, and honestly, with current real estate prices being so high, moving isn't practical.

4. Do you prefer living in a house or a flat?

I definitely prefer a house. Flats can feel a bit like a cramped bedsit, whereas a house gives you more freedom and privacy.

5. What part of your home do you like the most?

I absolutely love our balcony. It’s incredibly airy and a very cozy spot to unwind with a cup of coffee.

💡 IELTS Speaking Part 2 Guide

Format & Timing

  • • The Task: You will be given a cue card with a specific topic and prompts to follow.
  • • Prep Time (1 Min): You have exactly 1 minute to read the card and write down notes.
  • • Speaking (2 Mins): Speak continuously for up to 2 minutes until the examiner stops you.

How to Handle

  • • Smart Notes: Write keywords (verbs/nouns/adjectives) instead of full sentences to save time.
  • • Structure: Use the prompts as a roadmap (Intro, Body details, Conclusion).
  • • Fluency First: If you forget a detail, keep speaking! Fluency and pronunciation are more important than perfectly following the card.

🎙️ Part 2 Cue Card

1 Min Prep | 2 Mins Speak

Describe your dream home.

You should say:

  • Where the house would be
  • What the house would include
  • How it would look
  • And explain why you would like to live in this house
Recommended Sentence Starters:
  • a. When it comes to a house I would like to live in, I guess I would opt for...
  • b. I would kick off by saying something about the house's location...
  • c. Regarding what my dream house would include, I would say that...
  • d. As a final point, I'd like to mention the reasons why I would like to live in such a house...
💡 View P2 Sample Answer ▼
"When it comes to a house I would like to live in, I guess I would opt for a luxurious penthouse.

I would kick off by saying something about the house's location. It would ideally be situated right in the center of a bustling city, far above the typical noisy residential area.

Regarding what my dream house would include, I would say that it must feature an open-plan layout. I love the concept of minimalism, so the decor would be very simple, clean, and airy, strictly following the principles of Feng Shui to ensure good energy flow.

As a final point, I'd like to mention the reasons why I would like to live in such a house. Primarily, I despise feeling cramped in a tiny bedsit. A spacious penthouse would allow me to host amazing house-warming parties, and the massive windows would offer a truly cozy atmosphere while looking at the city lights."

Speaking Part 1

Practice: Countryside & City

💬 Part 1 Questions

  1. Would you like to live in the countryside in the future?
  2. What do people living in the countryside like to do?
  3. What are the benefits of living in rural areas?
  4. What's the difference between living in the city and living in the countryside?
💡 View Sample Answers ▼

1. Would you like to live in the countryside in the future?

Yes, definitely. When I retire, I would love to move to a peaceful rural area to escape the stressful rat race of the city.

2. What do people living in the countryside like to do?

They usually enjoy a very laid-back lifestyle. They like gardening, farming, and spending time outdoors rather than being obsessed with materialistic things.

3. What are the benefits of living in rural areas?

The main benefit is the higher standard of living in terms of mental health. The air is fresh, and the neighbors are usually very sincere and helpful, unlike in cities where people can sometimes be pretentious.

4. What's the difference between living in the city and living in the countryside?

The biggest difference is the pace of life. A metropolis is often plagued by horrible gridlock and noise. An urbanite is usually very diligent but stressed, whereas country life is much slower and relaxed.
