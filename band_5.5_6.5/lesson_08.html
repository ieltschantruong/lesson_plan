Listening Section

Section 3 Strategy Guide

👥 Section 3: Format & Overview

  • 1. Context: Educational or academic training situation (e.g., a university tutor discussing an assignment with students).
  • 2. Speakers: Up to 4 people interacting. Identifying who is speaking and what their opinion is is critical.
  • 3. Pace & Difficulty: The speed is generally faster, and the vocabulary is more academic and complex than Sections 1 and 2.

💡 Question Types Strategies

  • A. Multiple Choice (Many Answers): E.g., "Choose TWO letters A-E". Don't just listen for keywords; speakers will mention all options to distract you. Listen for agreement or final decisions between speakers.
  • B. Matching Features: You have a list of items and a list of options. The questions follow the order of the recording. Options do not. Use prep time to predict synonyms for the options.

Speaking Part 1

Colors (Practice)

COLORS

  1. What is your favorite color?
  2. Do you like dark colors?
  3. Do you usually wear clothes in your favorite color?
  4. Are there any colors you dislike?
💡 View Sample Answers ▼

1. What is your favorite color?

I'm incredibly fond of deep blue. It always gives me a sense of calmness and tranquility. I also enjoy soft pastel pinks because they convey a feeling of simplicity and youthfulness.

2. Do you like dark colors?

Yes, I actually do. I find that somber colors, especially black and dark greenish hues, convey a strong sense of power and mystery, making them perfect for formal attire.

3. Do you usually wear clothes in your favorite color?

Absolutely. I frequently wear matching outfits with vivid blue accessories. Since blue conveys such positive energy, wearing it instantly boosts my mood for the day.

4. Are there any colors you dislike?

I tend to avoid fluorescent colors, like neon yellow or bright orange. Even though they show vitality, they are too harsh on the eyes and don't really suit my personal style.

Speaking Part 1

Music (Practice)

MUSIC

  1. Do you like listening to music?
  2. When do you listen to music?
  3. Can you play any musical instruments?
  4. Did you have any music classes in school?
  5. Do you think it is necessary for children to have music classes?
💡 View Sample Answers ▼

1. Do you like listening to music?

Yes, absolutely. Music is my elixir. I have a diverse taste in music, ranging from classical to modern pop. It really helps me relax and creates a soothing ambience after a long day.

2. When do you listen to music?

I listen to music almost constantly—while commuting, studying, or even cooking. Whenever I hear a massive hit with a catchy tune, it completely energizes me.

3. Can you play any musical instruments?

Unfortunately, no. I am completely tone-deaf. I once tried to take up the guitar, but my rendition of even simple songs was terrible, so I gave it up.

4. Did you have any music classes in school?

Yes, I did. Music was a compulsory subject in our primary school curriculum. I vividly recall learning basic notes and singing in a choir.

5. Do you think it is necessary for children to have music classes?

I believe it is crucial. Learning an instrument helps children coordinate different parts of their body and improves their memory. Plus, discovering someone with a sweet singing voice at a young age is wonderful.

Speaking Part 2

Topic: Favorite Song (Cue Card)

🎧
📝

IELTS Cue Card

Describe your favorite song.

You should say:

  • What this song is about
  • When you listened to this song for the first time
  • How often you listen to this song
  • And explain why you think it is your favorite song.
💡 View Band 6.5+ Sample Answer ▼
"I would like to talk about a song that holds a very special place in my heart: 'Perfect' by Ed Sheeran. It's a beautiful, romantic ballad about young love and finding a partner you want to spend the rest of your life with. It is undeniably a massive hit globally.

To the best of my recollection, I first heard this song a few years ago during a friend's wedding. They chose it for their first dance. The moment the music started, the soothing ambience and Ed Sheeran's exceptionally sweet singing voice created a memory that remains engraved in my mind.

I listen to this song quite frequently, especially when I am commuting to work or when I need to relax after a stressful day. It acts as my personal elixir, instantly calming my nerves.

There are several reasons why this is my absolute favorite song. Firstly, it has a wonderfully catchy tune that is easy to hum along to, even though I am totally tone-deaf. Secondly, the lyrics are profound and deeply moving. Every time I hear a live rendition of it, I recall the warmth and joy of my friend's wedding day. It’s a timeless piece of music that never fails to lift my spirits."
