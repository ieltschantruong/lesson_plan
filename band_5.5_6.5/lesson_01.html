Speaking Part 1

Speaking Intro & Question Types

IELTS Speaking Overview & Part 1 Strategy

Test Format (11-14 Minutes)

  • Part 1: Introduction & Interview (4-5 mins). General questions about familiar topics (home, family, work, studies, interests).
  • Part 2: Long Turn (3-4 mins). Speak about a given topic on a cue card for 1-2 minutes.
  • Part 3: Two-way Discussion (4-5 mins). Abstract, more complex questions related to the Part 2 topic.

Marking Criteria

  • Fluency & Coherence: Speak smoothly without unnatural hesitation; connect ideas logically.
  • Lexical Resource: Use a wide range of vocabulary and idiomatic language accurately.
  • Grammatical Range & Accuracy: Use complex sentence structures with minimal errors.
  • Pronunciation: Clear articulation, intonation, and rhythm.

Part 1 Handling Strategies

  • Length of Answer: Aim for 2 to 4 sentences. Don't give one-word answers, but don't over-extend and go off-topic either.
  • A.R.E. Structure:
    Answer directly.
    Reason (say why).
    Example or Extra detail.
  • Keep it natural: Use conversational fillers (e.g., Well, actually... To be honest...) to sound native, but don't overuse them.
  • Paraphrase: Try to use synonyms rather than repeating the exact words from the examiner's question.

Example Answer:

"Do you like your hometown?"

"(A) Yes, absolutely. (R) It's a very peaceful place with a slow pace of life, which helps me relax. (E) For example, I can always enjoy a quiet walk by the river in the evenings without dealing with heavy traffic."

WOULD questions

For most of these questions, you should use the second conditional tense in your answer. In fact the examiner is expecting you to produce an example of this verb tense.

The form of the second conditional is quite simple:

If + S + V(past simple), S + would / could / might + V(infinitive).

Ex: If I lived near the sea, I would be able to eat fresh seafood.

Ex: If I had time, I would go for travelling.
