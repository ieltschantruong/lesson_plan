Speaking Questions

WEATHER

  1. What's your favorite season?
  2. What do people in your country like to do in different seasons?
  3. What's the least favored season in your country?
  4. What season is good for traveling in your country?
💡 View Sample Answers ▼

1. What's your favorite season?

I am definitely a warm weather person, so I love summer. However, I prefer it when it's just breezy rather than boiling hot.

2. What do people in your country like to do in different seasons?

In summer, when the weather is favorable, people enjoy outdoor activities. But during a dismal, drizzly winter day, everyone prefers to stay indoors to avoid the freezing cold.

3. What's the least favored season in your country?

It has to be the monsoon season. We frequently experience severe downpours that lead to terrible urban flooding, and getting drenched on the way to work is unbearable.

4. What season is good for traveling in your country?

Spring is ideal. The skies are rarely overcast, and you avoid the long heatwaves of summer, which means tourists are less likely to suffer from heatstroke.

Speaking Questions

PLANTS

  1. Do you like plants?
  2. What plants do you like?
  3. Have you ever grown any plants?
  4. Is having a garden popular in your country?
  5. What are the benefits of trees?
💡 View Sample Answers ▼

1. Do you like plants?

Yes, I love being surrounded by lush vegetation. It's incredibly relaxing to see the beautiful blossoms in the spring.

2. What plants do you like?

I really like evergreen trees because they stay vibrant year-round, unlike deciduous trees. I'm also quite fond of small ornamental plants like orchids.

3. Have you ever grown any plants?

Yes, though I wouldn't say I have a green thumb. I once tried to grow some tropical fruit trees, but they didn't survive the winter.

4. Is having a garden popular in your country?

It depends. In rural areas, many people have large fruit orchards. In cities, people try to maintain a small balcony garden to showcase local flora.

5. What are the benefits of trees?

They provide shady areas that are crucial during hot summers. Moreover, stately, old trees can significantly improve the air quality of a neighborhood.
