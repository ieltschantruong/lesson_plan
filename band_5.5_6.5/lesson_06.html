  2. What are the benefits of knowing how to cook?
  3. Do you like to try new food?
  4. What kinds of foreign food are popular in your country?
💡 View Sample Answers ▼
Water

1. What kinds of beverages do you like to drink?

I'm usually teetotal, so I don't drink alcohol. I prefer wholesome beverages like fruit juices, though I occasionally crave something sweet like bubble tea.

2. What are popular drinks in your country?

Iced coffee and fruit smoothies are very common. People often grab a bite and pair it with a refreshing drink to prevent dehydration in the heat.

3. How often do you drink water?

I have a habit of drinking water every hour. It's essential to get a sufficient amount so I don't feel fatigued.

4. What are the benefits of drinking enough water?

It helps detoxify the body and maintains our alertness. Without it, we can easily become lethargic.

Food & Cooking

1. Do you know how to cook?

Yes, I have basic culinary skills. My mother taught me how to make flavorful meals so I wouldn't have to rely on takeaways.

2. What are the benefits of knowing how to cook?

It helps you become more calorie-conscious because you control the ingredients, avoiding overly greasy fast food.

3. Do you like to try new food?

Definitely! I'm not a fussy eater, so I love exploring new dishes, whether they are spicy, sour, or salty.

4. What kinds of foreign food are popular in your country?

Korean and Japanese cuisines are huge right now. People love them because the dishes are vibrant and rarely bland.

Part 2: Special Event Food

Describe a kind of food people eat during a special event.

You should say:

  • What it is
  • How it is cooked
  • Why people cook this food
  • And explain why you like it
💡 View Full Sample Response ▼

a. What it is: When it comes to a type of food Vietnamese people often eat during a special event, I would immediately opt for Banh Chung, which is our traditional sticky rice cake served during the Lunar New Year.

b. How it is cooked: I would kick off by saying something about how it is prepared and cooked. Making it requires excellent culinary skills. The ingredients are quite wholesome, consisting of glutinous rice, mung beans, and fatty pork. They are carefully wrapped in green leaves and then boiled for about 10 to 12 hours. The process is so long that people used to feel quite fatigued watching the fire overnight, though nowadays it's easier.

c. Why people cook this food: Regarding why people cook this food, I have to explain that it's an essential tradition. In our culture, offering Banh Chung on the ancestral altar is a way to pay homage to our ancestors and pray for a sufficient and prosperous year ahead.

d. Explain why you like it: As a final point, I'd like to mention why I like this food. Honestly, I'm not a fussy eater, but I truly crave its flavorful taste. The combination of savory meat and soft rice is perfect, especially when paired with pickled onions to balance out the greasy pork. Although I try to be calorie-conscious since it's quite heavy, I always have a habit of enjoying a few slices during the holiday.
